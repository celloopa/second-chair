How to build it up

  • Loop bars 1 and 2 first. Em9 to A13 on its own is already the whole mood.
  • Bass slow, drums fast. One long note per bar, a slide into the next root, maybe an octave jump on beat 3.
  • Synth melody: try B, then A, then G, then F#, one note per bar, on a soft Vital bell or pad.
  • Guitar: skip strumming. Two clean notes at a time with lots of reverb, mostly G, B and F#.
  • Arrangement, about a minute: 8 bars of keys and guitar, then the break comes in for 16, then strip back and fade.
